2. Paste it into the reader

  1. On the reader: Home → File Transfer → Join Network. It shows a QR code and a short address like http://crosspoint.local/.
  2. On your phone: scan the QR (or type that address) to open the reader's own web page.
  3. There: OPDS Servers → Add, paste the link, Save.
  4. Back on the reader: leave File Transfer, then Home → OPDS → your library.

It opens straight on your Inbox — the books you have not downloaded yet. Open one to download it; it leaves the Inbox once the last byte lands. All Books and Recent sit at the bottom for anything you want back.

Notes

  • Firmware v1.3.0 or newer is required. Older builds run out of memory during the TLS handshake and simply fail to connect over HTTPS.
  • The link is the credential. Anyone holding it reads your shelf. It travels in the URL, so it does end up in server logs — that is the price of a setup with nothing to type. Lost the reader? Take a new link above; the old one dies at once.
  • A cancelled download stays in the Inbox. The book is marked delivered only when the last byte arrives.
  • EPUB only. Anything else is rejected at upload — the check reads the file's contents, so renaming a PDF will not sneak it through.
